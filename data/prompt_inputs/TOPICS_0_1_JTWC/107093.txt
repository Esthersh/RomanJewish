Source Text, 107093 (Latin):
Eos, qui in utero sunt, si nati sui heredes nobis futuri sunt, possumus 
instituere heredes: si quidem post mortem nostram nascantur, ex iure

English Translation:
If unborn children would be our proper heirs after their birth, we can appoint them heirs; if they are born after our death, we can make the appointment under the Civil Law; if born during our lifetime, we can do so by the Lex Junia.
