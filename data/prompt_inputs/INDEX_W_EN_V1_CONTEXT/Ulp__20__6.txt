Target Source Text, Ulp. 20, 6 (Latin): 
Pater et filius +  qui in potestate eius est, item duo fratres, qui in 
eiusdem patris potestate sunt, testes utrique, vel alter testis, alter

English Translation: 
A father, however, and a son who is under his control, as well as two brothers who are under the control of the same father, can both be witnesses; or one can be a witness, and the other the balance-holder, when a third party is the purchaser of the estate; as no harm results where several witnesses are taken from the same household in a transaction with a stranger.
