English Translation: 
If he left adult and minor daughters,
the adults are not provisioned at the expense of the minors,
and the minors are not maintained89 at the expense of the adults;
but rather, they divide equally.
If the adults married, the minors may marry.
And if the minors say: “Now, we are to be married in the manner that you were married”—
ones does not listen to them.
In this respect the rule is stricter for daughters than for sons,
that daughters are maintained at the expense of90 sons,
but are not maintained at the expense of daughters.
